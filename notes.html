Where the RH proof goes

Following references from proof and definition values in riemannHypothesis reaches 647,905 individually identified written declarations. The overlay places 640,546 of these across 16 arXiv subjects, including contextual assignments. The remaining 7,359 have source links in named sections. The three largest assigned subjects are number theory (175,450), commutative algebra (140,147) and rings and algebras (131,517).

One arithmetic construction supplies a local–global Galois parameter. On the analytic side, a Landau argument constrains poles using a window estimate. Another lemma passes from that estimate to a zero-free region. Each link opens a specific theorem where you can inspect the printed Lean statement and its proof dependencies.
