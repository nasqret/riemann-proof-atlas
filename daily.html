How the archive counts changes

A closed day takes the last first-parent revision of the official branch before midnight UTC. The first comparison starts with the submitted tree. A day can retain the same tree or have line edits with the same theorem count. The open day shows the time of its capture.

The atlas counts explicitly written Lean theorem and lemma commands in src/, excluding the challenge specification. Each daily comparison joins occurrences in two Git trees. Unique moves are recorded separately. Repeated names that cannot be resolved are flagged. A renamed declaration may appear among both removals and additions. New source totals and formal verification are different measurements.

The PR uses its own merge base. VerifyTask’s reading guide, source mirror and contributor proposals each identify their provenance and units. Download the official snapshot manifest.
